FlowScore — UI review

A review of every screen and the main navigation flows in the FlowScore app (1.4.0). Each board puts today's screen next to a proposed redesign, with numbered notes on what changes and why.

Main themes

  1. The app doesn't use its own colours. Coral from the icon becomes the accent for actions, selection and the tab bar. Green and the radio icon stay the "live" signal. Blue, which coral replaces as the main colour, moves to "Final" results.
  2. Real data breaks the layouts. Results columns truncate. Event names like "DDSR 11-12, 12-15, 16+, 30+…" wrap to four lines. Competition titles wrap to two lines.
  3. There's too much header. The competition header stays on top of every detail screen. Its ✕ means "switch competition", not "close".
  4. Floating overlays hide content. The live-now pill covers rows on every tab, and the "Back to…" banner covers the top of the heat list.
  5. Signed-out and error states feel unfinished. The home screen has no logo or app name, the Me tab never asks you to sign in, and each tab fails differently on a missing competition.

Bugs found while capturing

  • Schedule renders blank on Android (production 1.4.0, on a competition with a live session). No day tabs, list or empty state, and no errors in logcat.
  • The account dropdown stays open after deep-link navigation and sits on top of other screens.
  • The OAuth redirect route sends you to a 404. It still targets /(tabs), which no longer exists.
  • Dark-mode cards glow white because the card shadow uses the text colour.

Suggested order

  1. Quick wins: the dark-mode glow, the error and empty states (07), and removing the extra header on detail screens.
  2. Overlays and Schedule: dock the live pill (11) and fix the blank Schedule.
  3. Core screens: Schedule, heats and results (02–05).
  4. Everything else: the home screen, athlete page, settings and account menu.

Colour roles

Coral is the accent; filled buttons use #C24E22 so white text passes contrast. Green with the radio icon means live and nothing else. Blue marks final results. Purple and amber stay for Competing and Judging. Gold, silver and bronze mark the top three in results.

Sources for "current": the iOS simulator (signed out, main), a Pixel 8 running production 1.4.0 against a real competition (names blurred), and the April App Store screenshots, which use seeded data.
